# Engineering audit — rocket_engine_concept_v1

Status: E1 architecture / parametric visualization demonstrator.

## Defined
- subsystem decomposition
- common millimetre coordinate system
- provisional external envelopes
- parametric chamber/nozzle/pump skeleton
- requested external, cutaway, exploded and detail views

## Not validated
- thrust or specific impulse
- chamber pressure
- mixture ratio
- turbomachinery speeds
- injector stability
- combustion stability
- thermal margins
- cooling-channel sizing
- material allowables
- structural margins
- fatigue/life
- manufacturing tolerances
- propellant compatibility

## Red-team findings
The model must not be represented as a manufacturable or flight-qualified engine. The geometry is sufficient to establish visual hierarchy and a CAD data model, but not to support propulsion-performance or safety claims.

## Rendering brief
Show a plausible high-end engineering digital twin rather than a fictional marketing infographic. Preserve subsystem hierarchy: chamber and bell on centerline; twin turbopump modules above/alongside chamber; routed feed lines; valve block; gimbal frame; injector interface; visible regenerative cooling jacket in cutaway. Include an exploded view and sectional details. Avoid invented performance numbers. Label dimensions as PROVISIONAL CONCEPT GEOMETRY.
